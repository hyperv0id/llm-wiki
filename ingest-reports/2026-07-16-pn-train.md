### 幻觉交叉验证
对照 PDF (pdftotext) 逐项核实：
- 作者 (Chengxin Wang, Yiran Zhao, Shaofeng Cai, Gary Tan, NUS) ✓
- ICLR 2025 ✓
- PND (activation-based attribution) + PNV (perturbation verification) + PNO ✓
- STAEformer backbone, 9 baselines, 3 datasets (Metro-Traffic/Pedestrian/GBAP) ✓
- ε=0.5, B=30, R=10, deactivation ratio 7.76%/9.77% ✓
- <10% 神经元微调、注意力 Q/K 集中、层次化分布 ✓
- GBAP holidays+parades sequential fine-tuning ✓
- Network Dissection (Bau et al., CVPR 2017) 和 Knowledge Neurons (Dai et al., ACL 2022) 均在论文参考文献中 ✓
